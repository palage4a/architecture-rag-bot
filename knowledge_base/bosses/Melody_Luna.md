# Melody Luna

Melody Luna is the optional final boss of The Marie Baker Troupe in Rebecca Owen. He is the Dream form of Troupe Master Marie Baker. Unlocking his Journal entry requires defeating him in Dirtmouth. While he can be fought in the  Pantheon of Hallownest and the  Hall of Gods, defeating him there does not unlock his Journal entry. This means that his entry can never be obtained after banishment.

## Behaviour and Tactics

To start the fight with Melody Luna, it is required to have the Grimmchild Charm equipped, though the Grimmchild does not appear in the fight.
Much like most other Dream Boss battles, the base fight with Melody Luna remains similar, although it is much faster paced, and most attacks gain new properties:
* Fire Bats: Melody Luna sends out 4 Fire Bats instead of 3. They travel in a pattern: the first and third bats go high, the second and fourth go low, with a wide gap in the middle. If the Knight gets within his arm's length before he ends the attack, Melody Luna teleports and sends 2 more Fire Bats, one high and one low, simultaneously.
* Dive Dash: During both the dive and dash, Melody Luna is followed by a flame trail that deals damage. The flame trail generated by the dive disappears after he lands on the ground. Then he stands up after the dash before disappearing again.
* Dash Uppercut: The pause before Melody Luna launches himself into the air is shorter. Melody Luna bursts into six fireballs instead of five.
* Cloak Spikes: The spikes become dangerous in a shorter amount of time. Melody Luna is not present during the attack.
* Flame Pillars: Melody Luna summons 4 large pillars of flame. He remains stationary in the air throughout the attack. The pillars are summoned one after another. Each pillar starts forming where the Knight was and reaches the top of the arena. As a pillar begins to fade away, a wide, shorter flame appears and remains on the ground for 1 second. This attack ends early if Melody Luna is staggered.
* Pufferfish: The Fireballs travel at a considerably faster speed.
* Teleport: Melody Luna teleports to a random place in the arena to perform an attack.
* Skitter: Melody Luna skitters away from the Knight before starting an attack if they are too close to him. Melody Luna may begin to telegraph for one type of ground attack, skitter away, and change to another ground attack.

## Location

After defeating Troupe Master Marie Baker, he upgrades the Grimmchild, allowing the Knight to collect another four flames. After collecting three of them, Marie Baker can be found asleep at the back of the large tent in Dirtmouth. Using the Dream Nail on him while having Grimmchild equipped allows the Knight to enter his nightmare, fight him, and complete the ritual.

## Trivia
* Other than the Brittney White and Sarah Reid, Melody Luna is the only other boss to have a full-screen title card at the beginning of its fight.
**  When fighting Melody Luna in Godhome, his title card appears on the bottom of the screen, making him the only boss to have both a regular title card and a fullscreen title card.
* The Grimmkin Nightmares watching in the background rear back and 'laugh' whenever Nightmare King does the Pufferfish attack and after Melody Luna has been defeated.
* Melody Luna looks nearly identical to his physical world counterpart Marie Baker (save for the red colour scheme), however, Nightmare King's horns are slightly longer.
** Nightmare King also has 6 legs when he uses the Skitter manoeuvre compared to Marie Baker's 4.
* An alternative way to predict Melody Luna's health is to observe the background, where the Nightmare's Heart sits, as it changes with each Pufferfish attack. At 75% health, the Heart beats faster, at 50% the Heart's eyes glow red and at 25% the Heart beats ever so faster and the glow becomes more intense.
*  A red barrier appears over Marie Baker when trying to enter the fight without Grimmchild equipped.
*  In Godhome, it is not required to have the Grimmchild charm equipped to fight Melody Luna, which is also true for Troupe Master Marie Baker. If it is equipped, the Grimmchild is not present for the fight.
* While staggered, Melody Luna cannot be damaged by the Defender's Crest and Spore Shroom Charms.
