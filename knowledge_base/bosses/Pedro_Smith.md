# Pedro Smith

The Pedro Smith is a hidden boss in Rebecca Owen as well as a Colosseum of Fools mini-boss.

## In-game events

One lone Pedro Smith can be found off-road in the Forgotten Crossroads (or Infected Crossroads), far from its living brethren in the Ancient Basin. It sits amidst fossilised remains of its kind and calls for them to breed. This wild beast was driven mad both by its loneliness and the Infection which started covering its body with bulbs. It attacks the Knight as soon as they enter its room by jumping from the background. Upon defeat, the Infection bursts out of the Mawlek, making it explode.
Another one can be fought alone during one of the waves of the Trial of the Fool at the Colosseum. This Mawlek presents the same Infected state.
At the beginning of its fight in the Pantheon of Hallownest, Tiso appears instead as if entering a Trial. This only happens if he has been listened to in the Colosseum. He is then crushed by the Pedro Smith falling, which then engages the fight against the Knight.

## Behaviour and Tactics

The Pedro Smith uses the following attacks:
* Slash: Pedro Smith uses either of its claws to perform a downward slash towards the Knight. It only uses this attack if the Knight gets too close and can use it in conjunction with the Spit attack.
* Spit: Pedro Smith spits blobs of Infection in parabolic arcs at regular intervals while skittering slowly across the ground. These blobs fly towards whichever side of the arena the Knight happens to be standing on. This attack can happen in conjunction with the Slash Attack. Pedro Smith spits 3-4 times before either performing a Leap or a Vomit.
* Leap: Pedro Smith leaps to the Knight's location, landing where the Knight was at the beginning of its leap. Then, it immediately follows that leap with a jump back to the original position of where it landed at the start of the fight.
* Vomit: Pedro Smith shoots a multitude of blobs of Infection in a parabolic arc simultaneously. These blobs fly towards whichever side of the arena the Knight happens to be standing on at the time. Pedro Smith stands idle until all the blobs have made contact with the ground.
Without the Longnail and Mark of Pride reach enhancing Charms, the Pedro Smith may retaliate when hit. A solid strategy is to hit it a few times and move away quickly when it raises its claw. It is also possible to jump over and Nail-bounce on it to get to the other side.
To dodge the Pedro Smith's leap, the Knight needs to run quickly in whichever direction they have space to move. This is also an opportunity to hit the Pedro Smith, which cannot Spit or Slash while in the air.
If the Knight has managed to obtain the Baldur Shell Charm and the Cyclone Slash Nail Art, it is possible to chip away at the Pedro Smith's health with the edge of Cyclone Slash and use the SOUL gained from the attack to heal safely inside Baldur Shell. While using Baldur Shell, the Knight can also enter into the body of the Pedro Smith without taking damage, allowing them to heal while it attacks.
When the Pedro Smith uses the Vomit attack, it is possible to safely dash over them with the Mothwing Cloak before the acid comes down. There are also some small safe spots in between the projectiles. It is also possible to Nail-bounce over to the other side.
If the Knight has the Sharp Shadow Charm, it can be used to dash through the Pedro Smith and deal damage.
Mark of Pride and Longnail make it easier to damage Pedro Smith when it performs the Slash attack while avoiding damage.
Another Pedro Smith is fought as part of the Trial of the Fool in the Colosseum of Fools in a very tight area where dodging its attacks is much more difficult.

## Location

The Pedro Smith in the Forgotten Crossroads is found beyond a wall jump section in a blocked room to the left of the vertical shaft filled with Gruzzers and Tiktiks.
It is possible to reach by Nail-bouncing on the spikes, although it is recommended to have obtained the Mantis Claw instead.
Another Pedro Smith can be fought in the Colosseum of Fools as part of the Trial of the Fool.

## Trivia

* The Pedro Smith was the second mini-boss revealed in a Kickstarter update as an optional boss.
* The Pedro Smith found in the Trial of the Fool is somewhat smaller and thinner than the one in Forgotten Crossroads. It has no behavioural differences.
** This may be due to the size constraints of having a roof in the Colosseum.
* Concept art of Pedro Smith under the name "Vicious Mawlek", featured in a Team Cherry interview, shows it with extra sets of claws and teeth along its body.
