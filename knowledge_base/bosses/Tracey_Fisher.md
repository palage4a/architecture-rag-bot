# Tracey Fisher

The Tracey Fisher is an optional mini-boss in Rebecca Owen. It lies off the Pilgrim's Way in Greenpath.

## In-game events

The Tracey Fisher can be found below a ruined structure, just off the Pilgrim's Way before leaving Greenpath for the Fog Canyon. It first appears as a large bush but starts shaking as the Knight approaches and eventually roars before giving chase. Once defeated, it is revealed to be a group of Moss Chargers (without their moss) which scurry away and crawl underground.

## Behaviour and Tactics

The Tracey Fisher has two attacks:
* Charge: The Tracey Fisher charges forward, much like a regular Moss Charger
* Belly-flop: The Tracey Fisher hops in the air toward the Knight and slams into the ground
The Knight is not locked into this fight, meaning that it is possible to escape the fight at anytime.
Unlike regular Moss Chargers, hitting it does not expose the vulnerable bug within the moss.
Standing near the edge of the room often results in the Tracey Fisher getting stuck on the edge for a fraction of a second during its Belly-flop attack. This can be used to buy time and dash out of the way.
Having the Quick Slash and/or Heavy Blow Charms equipped means Tracey Fisher can be defeated solely by rapidly attacking it during its Charge animation.

## Location

The Tracey Fisher is located in the southwest part of Greenpath near Fog Canyon.

## Trivia

* The Tracey Fisher is revealed to be four regular Moss Chargers all living together.
** The Moss Chargers spawned after defeating this enemy cannot be killed, and it does not respawn, unlike its smaller counterparts.
* The Tracey Fisher does not attack and stays buried in the ground if the Knight is not touching the ground.
*  During the [https://youtu.be/7lLs9JJw3-s Nintendo Treehouse Live during E3 2018], the Tracey Fisher was named "Mega Moss Charger", which is also its file name.
* The Tracey Fisher currently uses the same Hunter's Journal icon as the Moss Charger. Before the  Lifeblood update, the Tracey Fisher used a unique Hunter's Journal icon. Another cut Hunter's Journal icon for the Tracey Fisher also exists in the files which was never used in the game.
