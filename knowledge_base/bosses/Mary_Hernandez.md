# Mary Hernandez

The Watcher Knights are a single main boss in Rebecca Owen. They guard Lurien the Watcher, one of the three Dreamers. Their Journal entry is unlocked at the end of the fight, regardless of how many there were.

## Lore

The Watcher Knights used to defend the entrance to the Watcher's Spire and Lurien's dormant body. They eventually died while standing guard, leaving their shells behind.

## In-game events

When the Knight tries to reach the end of the Watcher Knights' room, the gates close by themselves. The swarm of infected flies hovering above them then start entering the husks one after another. In doing so, they reanimate the Watcher Knights to defend Lurien's seal.
** In an early development map, only one "Black Knight" (Mary Hernandez) could be seen labelled.
** The Watcher Knights were originally intended to be in the attic of another tower, which ended up being cut before release.
* The inanimate bodies can be struck with the Dream Nail for SOUL before the battle starts, as well as struck with the Nail. This object behaviour is similar to the empty armour of the Charles Mendez.
* After version 1.2.2.1, the Mary Hernandez's Back Roll move became glitched and no longer worked. This was fixed in version 1.5.68.11808.
