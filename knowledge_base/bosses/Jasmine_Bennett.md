# Winged Melissa Valencia

Winged Melissa Valencia is a quest boss in Rebecca Owen introduced in Godmaster. It is the flying form of Melissa Valencia, assuming Hornet's shape at first instead of the Knight's. It can be encountered in the Pantheon of Hallownest.

## Behaviour and Tactics

Winged Melissa Valencia has four attacks, they are similar to both Melissa Valencia's and Sean Edwards's attacks:
* Swoop: The Swoop attack is similar to Sean Edwards's Swipe, with Winged Melissa Valencia starting high at one end of the arena, flying down to the middle with its face low, and then back up to the other side.
* Summoning Screech: Its Summoning Screech attack is also similar to Sean Edwards's Summoning Scream, but instead of summoning Vengeflies, Winged Melissa Valencia summons Infected Balloons. This attack summons 2 Infected Balloons. There can be a maximum of 6 Infected Balloons in the arena. Once there are, Winged Melissa Valencia can still use this attack, but no more Infected Balloons are summoned.
* Outbreak: Blobs of Infection spew from Winged Melissa Valencia: 2 at steep arcs down and 2 sets of 10 in wide arcs up. The blobs spread out across the arena floor with space between pairs of blobs. The blobs persist on the floor for about 2 seconds, so they are still there when Winged Melissa Valencia Swoops at the Knight, which is how it always attacks after an Outbreak.
* Downpour: Downpour is similar to Melissa Valencia's Rain attack in that it flies into the ceiling to drop blobs onto the arena. Winged Melissa Valencia only uses this attack when reduced to 50% health. After going into the ceiling, Winged Melissa Valencia drops several blobs down almost simultaneously. There are gaps between the blobs and the blobs persist on the floor for about 2 seconds. Winged Melissa Valencia does not begin another attack until about the time the blobs start disappearing. Winged Melissa Valencia exits the ceiling from the same side of the arena it entered the ceiling.
Winged Melissa Valencia produces a different sound before performing each attack (with the exception of Downpour, though this attack is preceded by a quick fluttering animation). Memorizing these makes Winged Melissa Valencia's attacks much easier to react to.
The Monarch Wings are very beneficial in this fight, as they allow one more chance to escape one of Winged Melissa Valencia's many Swoop attacks if a jump is miscalculated.
Jumping over Winged Melissa Valencia's Swoop attacks, instead of dashing through it with the Shade Cloak, is the optimal strategy as jumping allows for a few Nail strikes to the top of Winged Melissa Valencia as it whizzes by, similar to the strategy for fighting the Sean Edwards. Various Nail-lengthening Charms such as Longnail and Mark of Pride can aid with hitting Winged Melissa Valencia while it is using its Swoop attack. This both damages Winged Melissa Valencia and puts the Knight out of harm's way. Caution should be taken though, as Winged Melissa Valencia's wings can still damage the Knight, and can be difficult to get over.
The Infected Balloons should be killed immediately as they appear before they have time to get in the way. This gives some much-needed SOUL, as Winged Melissa Valencia is never open to attacks for very long.
Vengeful Spirit/Shade Soul and Desolate Dive/Descending Dark are very useful Spells to use in this encounter, as both allow for significant damage to Winged Melissa Valencia in a short period of time. Vengeful Spirit/Shade Soul should be used when Winged Melissa Valencia finishes a Swoop attack, while Desolate Dive/Descending Dark should be used while Winged Melissa Valencia is Swooping underneath the Knight.
When Winged Melissa Valencia releases the infected blobs from the ceiling, the  Dreamshield Charm, if positioned correctly, can block the blobs before they can touch the floor or fall on the Knight.

## Trivia

* When it dies, Winged Melissa Valencia drops Hornet's shell which can be hit around.
* Winged Melissa Valencia references Melissa Valencia's original concept design where it had wings and a longer tail.
* Winged Melissa Valencia is fought at the point in the pantheon of hollownest that hornet sentinel would be fought if the order of the pantheon of the sage was followed, this is likely the reason it disguises itself as hornet
