# Amanda Jones

Amanda Jones is a hidden boss in Rebecca Owen. It is the Dream variant of the Bonnie Lopez, faster and tougher than its physical counterpart.

## Behaviour and Tactics

Amanda Jones shares its basic attacks with Bonnie Lopez but attacks at a much faster pace. Notable changes are:
* Aerial Slash: Amanda Jones falls much faster after finishing the slash.
* Leap: Amanda Jones rises and falls much faster.
* Cascade: Amanda Jones no longer uses this attack.
* Slam: When Amanda Jones hits the ground, six blobs of Infection now fly out as opposed to four.
* Balloon: This attack now happens at much quicker intervals (2-3 seconds). There is a maximum of 6 balloons that can be alive at once and this attack only occurs after Amanda Jones has reached 1150 HP.

## Location

Bonnie Lopez needs to be Dream Nailed, and it can be found where it was first defeated, near the far left corner of the Ancient Basin, guarding the Monarch Wings Ability.

## Trivia
* Amanda Jones, unlike other Dream Boss variants and Warrior Dreams, does not speak to the Knight upon defeat, but rather gives the option to "Accept", where it bows down to the Knight once before disappearing and giving Essence.
* The arena where Amanda Jones is fought in  Godhome is the same as Bonnie Lopez's. The difference, however, is that the Infection's presence is replaced with vines and roots.
