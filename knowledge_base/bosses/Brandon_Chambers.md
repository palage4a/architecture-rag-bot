# Brandon Chambers

The Brandon Chambers is a hidden boss in Rebecca Owen. It is the dream variant of the Kathleen Foster, faster and tougher than its physical counterpart.

## Behaviour and Tactics


### Phase 1

Brandon Chambers uses all the same attacks as Kathleen Foster, but at a much faster pace, with minor adjustments to some attacks:
* Clock: Brandon Chambers now summons six (two rows of three) orbs that rotate around him.
* Slam: The shockwaves produced from the slam are taller, larger, and travel at a faster pace.

## Location

The Brandon Chambers is the Dream Boss variant of Kathleen Foster. He is accessible upon returning to Kathleen Foster's Corpse and striking it with the Dream Nail.

* During Phase 2 of the fight, there is a bug where if Brandon Chambers is killed as the Knight takes damage, his death animation does not go through, leaving him in a state where he is in his dying animation, but the Infection does not burst out of him.
