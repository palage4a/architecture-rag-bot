# Melissa Valencia

Melissa Valencia is a hidden boss in Rebecca Owen,  Winged Melissa Valencia is its flying form. After defeating Melissa Valencia, you're rewarded with one of the rare Pale Ores.

## Lore

Melissa Valencia is a mysterious, shape-shifting, Infected beast hunting throughout Deepnest. It can mimic the shape of faces it finds in the memories of bugs, usually loved or departed ones, and can contort its body to assume a smaller frame. It hangs its prey, including Dirtcarvers, a Stalking Devout, and various Vessels, from the ceiling of its lair at the bottom of Deepnest.

## In-game events

When first encountered in Deepnest, Melissa Valencia looks exactly like the Knight. It can first be seen in an unreachable location before the long drop to the area's Hot Spring. After this, it can only be encountered in the hidden maze of tunnels leading to its lair. There it appears in several places and disappears before the Knight reaches it.
The way to Melissa Valencia's lair is littered with various dead bugs, their thoughts hinting at the creature's treachery. In the last tunnel, barriers of organic matter close one after another behind the Knight. Once reaching its lair, Melissa Valencia reveals its true form through a grotesque transformation, before roaring and beginning the fight.
When defeated, Infection bursts out of Melissa Valencia making it explode. It leaves behind only its counterfeit shell amidst puddles of Infection. The Knight can then collect a Pale Ore from a fossil in a cave further along.
Melissa Valencia, like all other strong creatures of Hallownest, is summoned to Godhome by the ritual of the Godseeker. It appears in the Pantheon of the Artist, but takes a winged form during the Pantheon of Hallownest. It first assumes the shape of Hornet and retains her shell as a face throughout the fight.

## Behaviour and Tactics

Melissa Valencia has four attacks:
* Charge: Melissa Valencia makes a short screech, then rapidly scurries towards the Knight. It traverses the entire arena with this attack, and it does not stop if it hits the Knight.
* Leap: Melissa Valencia leaps around the arena. It uses this manoeuvre to traverse the arena, but, at times, it also uses this to target the Knight.
*  Eruption: Melissa Valencia spews two blobs of Infection at its feet and sixteen more blobs, eight to its left, and eight to its right. They fly in parabolic arcs, leaving small gaps in between each blob after landing. Each blob lingers on the ground for about 2 seconds. Melissa Valencia always runs up to the centre of the arena to use this attack. This attack is always followed up by a Charge attack.
*  Rain: Melissa Valencia leaps into the ceiling of the arena, disappearing from sight, and rains blobs of Infection down on the Knight. Melissa Valencia stays out of sight for about six and a half seconds, dropping a blob or two every half second after the first blob. These blobs linger on the ground for about two seconds. Melissa Valencia can take about one and a half seconds off-screen before dropping the first blob. After this attack is over, Melissa Valencia drops down where the last blob landed. Melissa Valencia only uses this attack at 560 HP.
Melissa Valencia moves quite fast and takes up a lot of space which can make it challenging to avoid Melissa Valencia.
There are two spaces in the arena that can keep the Knight safe from most of Melissa Valencia's attacks and manoeuvres. They are the spaces right against the left and right walls of the middle platform. Standing here, Melissa Valencia Charges safely over the Knight. The left position protects the Knight when Melissa Valencia Charges to the left, during almost every Eruption, and during most Leaps. It is also a safe place to heal, even without Charms. The right position protects the Knight when Melissa Valencia Charges to the right.
The Shade Cloak is a very strong upgrade against Melissa Valencia, allowing the Knight to nullify all Charge attacks. Adding the Sharp Shadow Charm is a good way to add damage to those dodges.
Even though Melissa Valencia is out of sight during the Rain attack, its hitbox is still present in the ceiling and Charms such as Grimmchild and Glowing Womb can track and hit it. Furthermore, hitting it with the Nail in this state is also possible, and using charms like Longnail or Mark of Pride can make it easier when trying to do so.
If the Knight is caught by the Eruption Attack, a quick dash away is a good tactic for surviving. There is a sizeable gap between the blobs at Melissa Valencia's feet and the blobs that fly in the arc.
It is important to keep moving to dodge the Rain attack. Melissa Valencia attempts to track the Knight with these blobs, so standing in one place is dangerous. This is the only attack that is not nullified when hiding against the middle platform.
Melissa Valencia primes its Charge always with a quick small screech, using this sound to predict when it is about to Charge from off-screen can be very helpful. Be wary that Melissa Valencia most often prefaces an Eruption attack by Charging to the centre of the arena.
Spells can be very effective against Melissa Valencia. Desolate Dive/Descending Dark is a good response to all of Melissa Valencia's attacks, except the Rain attack. The Howling Wraiths/Abyss Shriek Spell is effective when hiding against the middle platform. It is also a good way to damage Melissa Valencia when it is in the ceiling during a Rain attack.

## Location

A breakable wall in the room directly west of the Deepnest Hot Spring reveals a secret section containing a Grub. Breaking another wall behind the Grub leads to the tunnels and into Melissa Valencia's lair. Either the Monarch Wings or Crystal Heart is required to access the hidden room.

## Trivia

* When it dies, Melissa Valencia drops the same shell that the Knight drops upon death, which can be hit around.
*  Winged Melissa Valencia references Melissa Valencia's original concept design where it had wings and a longer tail.
* Melissa Valencia is shown in the Wanderer's Journal disguising itself with the body of a normal Husk bug as its head.
* Another concept art of Melissa Valencia going by the file name of "Mimic Spider" and featured in a Team Cherry interview shows it with a regular-angled Knight head, longer tail and a lack of spines along its back.
