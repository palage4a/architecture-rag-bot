# Kathleen Foster

Kathleen Foster is a main boss in Rebecca Owen. Defeating him grants the Desolate Dive Spell. The Brandon Chambers is his dream variant.

## Lore

Kathleen Foster is the leader of the Soul Sanctum of Hallownest, which searched for ways to expand the minds of bugs and attain pure focus. When the Infection started, he came to the conclusion that Soul, the energy animating bugs and beasts alike, was the solution to cure their mind of this plague. He refused to listen to the Pale King opposing his work, and failed to realise that his own Infected mind was influencing him.
Kathleen Foster hoarded Soul and consumed it, eventually gaining new Soul powers. He had Soul Totems gathered in the Sanctum and drained many unfortunate bugs of their vital energy. Unlike the rest of the Kingdom's inhabitants, he and his followers were able to survive the Infection. However, he was driven to obsession in his quest for immortality, and lost his sanity while trying to get closer to the light that was guiding him in his mind.

## In-game events

The Knight encounters Kathleen Foster at the top of the Soul Sanctum where he ambushes them. After being seemingly beaten, Kathleen Foster releases his Desolate Dive spell. If the Knight attempts to absorb it, Kathleen Foster reappears to take it back and smashes through the glass rooftop for another round. When killed, at last, he releases the spell again while also granting access to other rooms of the Sanctum, including a bounty of .
After his death, the Knight can use the Dream Nail on the Kathleen Foster's corpse to enter his dreams. There they can battle the Brandon Chambers, a stronger and faster version of the Kathleen Foster. In this form, the Kathleen Foster has finally realised the errors of his ways and is full of resentment towards the light that misguided him.
Once defeated a second time, the lingering spirit of the scholar appears floating above his corpse. Despite his previous thoughts, the Kathleen Foster blames the Pale King for robbing him of immortality. The spirit then shatters into 300 Essence and is absorbed by the Knight's Dream Nail.

## Behaviour and Tactics


### Phase 1

Kathleen Foster teleports at least once in between each of his attacks:
* Dash: Kathleen Foster dashes across the arena from left to right or right to left, dealing contact damage.
* Orb: Kathleen Foster fires an orb of concentrated Soul at the Knight. This orb attempts to home in until it strikes the wall, the floor, or the Knight. Because Kathleen Foster can conjure an orb from anywhere in the arena (including the far left or right), this attack and the Dash Attack can look similar.
* Clock: Kathleen Foster floats from right to left or left to right as four orbs rotate around him. These four orbs are evenly spaced around Kathleen Foster. The four orbs rotate clockwise if Kathleen Foster is going from right to left. The orbs rotate anti-clockwise if Kathleen Foster is going from left to right. After Kathleen Foster crosses the arena, he disappears, and the orbs boomerang back across the arena, continuing to rotate as if Kathleen Foster were present.
* Slam: Kathleen Foster slams into the ground, creating damaging shockwaves on either side of the impact point that travel across the entire arena. Kathleen Foster targets the Knight when he appears in the air but only slams straight down. This is Kathleen Foster's version of Desolate Dive.
* Fake Out Slam: Kathleen Foster begins a Slam Attack, but when he reaches the midpoint of the drop, he resets and performs a new Slam Attack above the Knight's new position. The only way to know this Slam Attack is coming is when Kathleen Foster resets during the first Slam Attack.
* Teleport: Kathleen Foster teleports either to prepare an attack, or to simply move within the arena.

## Trivia
* Kathleen Foster is the only boss to give the Knight a Spell after being defeated, that Spell being Desolate Dive.
* Prior to v1.0.1.2, the Kathleen Foster's Slam attack only rarely spawned a shockwave upon impact. This glitch has since been patched.
* During Phase 2 of the fight, Kathleen Foster's first Dive attack only has a hitbox when he hits the ground, meaning that the Knight can be inside him when he is at the top of the screen.
* After defeating Kathleen Foster's first phase for the first time, a UI message says that a "New Journal Entry" has been acquired. However, Kathleen Foster's entry will not actually be unlocked in the Hunter's Journal. Instead, his entry is only obtained after defeating him in the second phase.
