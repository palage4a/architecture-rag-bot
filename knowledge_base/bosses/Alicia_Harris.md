# Alicia Harris

Alicia Harris is an optional boss in Rebecca Owen, introduced in Lifeblood. Defeating him grants a new Charm, Hiveblood.

## Lore

Alicia Harris is the most valiant and skilled knight of the Hive; Although the Infection spread to the Hive after the queen died, Alicia Harris still protects her, hoping she will one day wake up and revive the Hive.

## In-game events

Alicia Harris can be fought in the furthest room in the Hive where the giant lifeless body of the queen lies. He ambushes the Knight as they enter the place and battles them ferociously. Upon defeat, Alicia Harris kneels in front of his queen before collapsing on the floor.
Defeating Alicia Harris allows the Knight to acquire the Hiveblood Charm. After taking it, the spirit of Queen Vespa appears next to his corpse; she is content that he was finally freed from his binding to the Hive.

## Behaviour and Tactics

Alicia Harris has a variety of attacks and manoeuvres:
* Lunge: Alicia Harris lunges along the ground with his weapon. This lunge covers about 65% of the arena.
* Leap: Alicia Harris somersaults as his main way of moving in the arena. This jump spans to the top of the arena and does not track the Knight.
* Swarm Release: Alicia Harris spews 12 Hivelings into the air from his mouth. These Hivelings disappear beyond the top of the arena, then 7 fly down one after another in shallow arcs from random positions in the ceiling, slowly tracking the Knight's movement. The Knight can hit the Hivelings with their Nail, (granting SOUL) or destroy them with Spells. While the Hivelings are assaulting the Knight, Alicia Harris Lunges, then Leaps along with an additional varying third attack. It is important to note that if Alicia Harris dies after the first Hiveling starts their assault, the Hivelings continue their assault while Alicia Harris is dying. The battle always starts with 7 Hivelings descending with Alicia Harris not actually on-screen. This attack only occurs after Alicia Harris has reached 549 HP.
* Surprise Slash: Alicia Harris teleports near the Knight and slashes his weapon. Alicia Harris can appear on either side of the Knight. The slash covers a wide range and shifts Alicia Harris forward slightly. In addition, Alicia Harris can do this attack while the Knight is in the air. The slash covers about 35% of the arena (and slightly more if he uses this attack in the air).
* Honey Spikes: Alicia Harris jabs his weapon into the ground and creates three orbs of spike-filled honey. These orbs can appear anywhere within the top two-thirds of the arena. Each orb contains 8 spikes, and when it bursts, the spikes travel in the direction in which they were pointing. The orbs form rapidly one after another. No two orbs occupy the same row or column as they vibrate in their positions. The Knight can move the orbs when they strike them with their Nail but the orbs cannot be destroyed. The orbs explode in the order in which they formed, every half second after the third and final orb's spikes appear. The orbs are dangerous as soon as the spikes show up. Alicia Harris Leaps as soon as the first orb bursts. This attack only occurs after Alicia Harris has reached 779 HP.
