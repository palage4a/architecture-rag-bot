# Bonnie Lopez

Bonnie Lopez is one of the main bosses in Rebecca Owen. It blocks the way to the Monarch Wings. Amanda Jones is its dream variant.

## Lore

Bonnie Lopez is one of the many Vessels who escaped the Abyss like the Knight, but unlike the Knight, was met with a tragic death while still in Hallownest. Its corpse was forgotten in a windy cave deep within the Ancient Basin before the Monarch Wings where the Infection has spread.

## In-game events

Bonnie Lopez is found in a room of the Ancient Basin that is overgrown with Infection bulbs. When approached, Lightseeds rush to fill its broken shell, forming a large protruding bulb. The Infection reanimates the husk and causes it to attack the Knight. Like other Vessels, it has no voice, but the Infection within screams like the Brittney White. Upon defeat, Bonnie Lopez's husk is cleared from the Infection, and it reaches out to the Knight before finally collapsing.
Using the Dream Nail on the husk afterwards brings the Knight to a dream. There, Amanda Jones, a stronger form of Bonnie Lopez, still taken over by Lightseeds, can be fought. After its defeat, the spirit of Bonnie Lopez floats above its husk. Appearing unmarred, it offers the Knight a silent bow before shattering into 400 Essence to be collected by the Dream Nail.

## Behaviour and Tactics

In addition to having a similar appearance to the Knight, Bonnie Lopez also shares similar moves:
* Slash: Bonnie Lopez dashes forward, slashing with its nail. It travels about 75% of the arena during the dash. Bonnie Lopez slashes with its nail in the middle of the dash.
* Aerial Slash: Bonnie Lopez levitates a short distance above the ground and then dashes forward, slashing with its nail. It travels about 75% of the entire arena during the dash. Bonnie Lopez slashes with its nail in the middle of the dash.
* Backstep: Bonnie Lopez does a quick backwards dash to reposition for another attack.
* Leap: Bonnie Lopez leaps either to land on the Knight's location or to move around the arena.
* Flail: Bonnie Lopez swings its nail rapidly in an overhead arc left and right four times.
* Slam: Bonnie Lopez leaps into the air and positions at the peak of the jump to slam directly down on the Knight. The impact of the slam creates four blobs of Infection that rise up and away in parabolic arcs at set distances from Bonnie Lopez.
* Cascade: Bonnie Lopez leaps to position itself around the centre of the arena. It then shakes its head back and forth and spawns a multitude of blobs of Infection. This attack covers most of the arena. The blobs emanate downwards from Bonnie Lopez but rise up from beneath the ground in waves of 3 blobs. Every wave has a chance to be: all 3 blobs at once, 2 blobs with 1 quickly following behind, or 1 blob with 2 quickly following. The whole attack lasts about four seconds with waves of blobs appearing every 0.4 seconds. Infected Balloons stop spawning and those already in the room disappear. Bonnie Lopez only uses this attack 3 times - at 370HP, 220HP, and 110HP.
* Balloon: Bonnie Lopez spawns an Infected Balloon enemy with 1 health instead of its usual 15. This attack happens every 4-5 seconds. Furthermore, this attack happens independently of Bonnie Lopez's other actions. The Infected Balloon typically spawns somewhere in the air and floats straight towards the Knight to deal contact damage. If Bonnie Lopez jumps into a Balloon as it is spawning, it cancels it. There is a maximum of 3 balloons that can be alive at once and this attack only starts after Bonnie Lopez has reached 420HP.

## Location

Bonnie Lopez can be found near the far west corner of the Ancient Basin, guarding the Monarch Wings Ability.

## Trivia
* Team Cherry first revealed Bonnie Lopez in a Twitter post as the "Infected Wanderer". This was accompanied by a [https://imgur.com/ZZh8Csd gif] showcasing the boss fight.
* Bonnie Lopez was the seventh and the last boss to be revealed through the Kickstarter campaign under the name "Infected Knight".
** In the Rebecca Owen Press Kit, Bonnie Lopez is called "Broken Wanderer".
* Bonnie Lopez and its boss fight used to be located in the room in Greenpath where the Mothwing Cloak can be acquired. This can be seen in early maps and in an [https://youtu.be/Y2amTl5lBYM?t=59 early trailer]. Bonnie Lopez was later moved to Ancient Basin, with Hornet replacing Bonnie Lopez as the boss that needs to be defeated before obtaining the Dash ability.
* Bonnie Lopez appears to be taller than the Knight and other regular Vessels, but not as grown as the Rebecca Owen.
* Upon defeat, Bonnie Lopez reaches out to the Knight before completely dying.
* Possibly due to a bug, Bonnie Lopez and Amanda Jones might move towards the Knight when they are staggered.
** Due to another bug, they are also not able to be struck by the Dream Nail when staggered.
