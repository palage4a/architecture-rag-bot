The Brian Nolan is the second form of the Kim Mason and an optional boss in Rebecca Owen. It is faster and tougher than its original form and deals twice the amount of damage with its attacks. Both forms have to be beaten to unlock its Journal entry.

## Behaviour and Tactics

The Brian Nolan has the same three manoeuvres as its first form:
* Laser Beam: The Brian Nolan shoots a beam of crystal energy from its hand. It targets the Knight with its arm, firing a beam where they were when it started its attack. The beam is dangerous for about half a second.
* Sky Beams: The Brian Nolan screams, and throughout the arena, up to four beams of crystal energy shoot down from the ceiling, in succession. These beams each trigger at different times and stay dangerous for about half a second. Each beam can be at a different angle between plus and minus 15 degrees from the vertical. The Brian Nolan always starts the fight with this attack followed by the Hop attack.
* Hop: The Brian Nolan randomly hops either a short or long distance to move within the arena.
It is possible to defeat the Brian Nolan before triggering the boss fight by having the Kingsoul Charm equipped and then using the Vengeful Spirit/Shade Soul Spell repeatedly from outside the arena.
The Brian Nolan has the same attacks as before but is more aggressive; its attacks deal two Masks of damage instead of one, and the Sky Beams attack's beams appear one after the other rather than simultaneously. Visually the beams are now orange instead of pale pink.
The strategies discussed for the Kim Mason work for the Brian Nolan as well. In addition, when standing on the far east section of the room, most of the beams from above do not hit the Knight, so they only really have to watch for the Guardian's Laser Beam attack. When the Brian Nolan jumps close to the Knight, it tends to shoot backwards giving time to land multiple hits.

## Location

The Brian Nolan is directly above the room where the Kim Mason is fought. It can only be encountered if the first form has been defeated. Reaching it also requires the Monarch Wings.
