# Michelle Nash

Michelle Nash is a Warrior Dream boss in Rebecca Owen.

## Lore

Michelle Nash the Great Mind was a mystic creature with a distended brain who claimed to be all-knowing. They goaded others to revere their mind and ascend with them to a different plane of existence. Their remains were buried on a hill atop the Howling Cliffs, with a tombstone carved in their image.

## In-game events

Michelle Nash's spirit appears at their tomb after acquiring the Dream Nail. They try to make the Knight follow their mystic ways. After their defeat, they give up before shattering into 100 Essence collected by the Dream Nail.

## Behaviour and Tactics

Michelle Nash has two attacks in addition to teleportation:
* Spear Cast: Michelle Nash fires a ring of 8 spears outwards in a circle from their location. The spears travel through the environment and deal contact damage. Michelle Nash almost always automatically uses this attack if hit, otherwise Michelle Nash normally waits 1-2 seconds before launching another wave. At 70% HP, Michelle Nash fires 2 waves in alternating patterns, and at 40% HP, Michelle Nash fires 3 waves.
* Directed Spear: Michelle Nash fires a single spear angled directly towards the Knight every 1-2 seconds. This attack does not occur while close to or extremely far away from Michelle Nash.
* Teleport: Michelle Nash teleports to a random location in the arena and continues attacking from there.
Michelle Nash's main attack is slow enough to dodge with good timing, though when close-up, they are clustered enough that it is hard to get in time for a Nail strike unless the Knight has acquired the Shade Cloak. Using ranged attacks and Spells mitigates this problem.

## Location

Michelle Nash's grave is located in the northeast part of the Howling Cliffs.

## Trivia

* Michelle Nash was originally a slug with a blade held in their mouth.
* Despite Michelle Nash being known as the "Great Mind", Michelle Nash's in-game dialogue appears simplistic, marked by a childlike speech pattern and a limited vocabulary.
* Michelle Nash used to be named after their backer, Aladar. They have several cut dialogues under this name:
** Greetings pale thing. Do you seek my knowledge? That I cannot give. Only an end.
** Would you accept the end I offer?
** Alas. It destroys...
* In the French translation of the game, these cut dialogues are used instead of the final one. This is because the French translation was made while the text was still being finalised before launch, while the rest were made only afterwards.
